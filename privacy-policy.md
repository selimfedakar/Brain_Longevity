## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
- Display name, email address, and a bcrypt-hashed password (never stored in plain text)
- Selected mode (Biohacker or Longevity)
- Date of account creation and last activity

### 2.2 Health Profile (entered voluntarily during onboarding)
- Age, biological sex
- Cardiovascular risk factor (self-reported, 1–5 scale)
- Whether you have hearing loss, diabetes, or hypertension
- Social isolation self-assessment score
- Education level (years)
- Baseline cognitive score

### 2.3 Apple HealthKit Data
With your explicit permission we read:
- Sleep duration and sleep analysis
- Exercise minutes and active energy
- Heart rate data

HealthKit data is accessed **read-only**, processed on-device or on our backend solely to compute your Brain Health Index (BHI), and is **never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sold.**

### 2.4 Voice Recordings
When you use the Voice Journal feature:
- Your audio recording is captured via the device microphone
- The recording is transmitted to **OpenAI Whisper API** for transcription
- The transcript and computed speech-fluency metrics are stored on our servers
- The original audio file is discarded after transcription

Voice data is used exclusively for cognitive fluency assessment. It is never used for advertising or shared beyond the transcription service.

### 2.5 Cognitive Assessment Results
- Game session data from Stroop, N-Back, and Trail Making tests
- Response times, accuracy percentages, and session metadata

### 2.6 Dietary Tracking Data
- MIND diet checklist responses you enter manually

### 2.7 Social Features (opt-in)
- Your display name and BHI score are visible to accepted friends on the leaderboard
- Friend connections require mutual consent

### 2.8 Technical Data
- Device type, operating system version (for diagnostics)
- Anonymous app usage logs (feature usage frequency, session lengths)

## 7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and KVKK you have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure** — Delete your account and all associated data (available in-app — see Section 8)
- **Data Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Restriction** — Request that we restrict processing of your data
- **Objection** — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdrawal of Consent** — Revoke HealthKit or microphone access at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy

To exercise any of these rights, email us at: **a.selimfedakar@gmail.com**  
We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 8. Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

You can permanently delete your account from within the app:

**Home Tab → Profile Section → Delete Account**

Account deletion:
- Removes all personal data, cognitive sessions, voice records, diet logs, and social connections from our servers
- Takes effect immediately; data is fully purged within 30 days
- Cannot be undone
- Does not affect data stored in Apple Health (managed separately by Apple)

BrainLongevity is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately at a.selimfedakar@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

## 10. Security

We implement industry-standard security practices:

- Passwords hashed with bcrypt (salted, never stored in plain text)
- All data transmitted over HTTPS / TLS 1.2+
- Authentication via short-lived JWT access tokens + secure refresh tokens
- Refresh tokens stored in iOS Secure Enclave (expo-secure-store)
- Database access restricted to backend services via private network
